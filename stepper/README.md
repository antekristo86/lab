# jelly stepper

A stepper made of liquid. The value lives behind the glass on a wheel.
The minus and the plus are not buttons that appear, they are droplets
born out of the body and swallowed back into it at the ends of the
range, through the same neck that tears in the jelly glass drop.

Nothing fades and nothing is keyframed. The tearing, the healing and
the wobble all fall out of one smooth union and a handful of springs.

- 0 to 5. At 0 there is no minus, at 5 there is no plus.
- Press the signs, or use the arrow keys, or the mouse wheel.
- The body can be picked up and thrown. The row trails behind it.

One file, no dependencies. The lens, the refraction and the shadow are
one fragment shader.

    npx degit antekristo86/lab/stepper
