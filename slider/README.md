# jelly slider

The jelly glass button, on a rail. The rail is a scale of thin
vertical strokes, lit behind the body, faint ahead of it. The body sits
on the scale like a bead on a wire and can be dragged along it. The
value lives behind the glass on a wheel and clicks past every slot the
body crosses, 0 on the left, 10 on the right. Let go and the body finds
the nearest slot on its own springs and overshoots it.

One fragment shader for the glass, springs for everything else. No
keyframes, no easing curves.

- `demo.html`, the whole thing. Open it.
